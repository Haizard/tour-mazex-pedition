Kilimanjaro Climb

With no doubt, Mt Kilimanjaro is Africa’s leading tourist attraction. As experienced professionals, we only recommend four routes on Kilimanjaro the Lemosho Route, Machame Route, Marangu Route, and Northern Circuit Route. This is because of their uniqueness compared to other routes; moreover, these routes are suitable for all types of travelers.

Cultural Tours

Cultural tourism allows local people such as Maasai, chagga, sukuma, makonde, pare, hadzabe, datoga, nyamwezi, wazaramo and many others to offer tours that show their culture, their sacred places of worship, economic activities, for example, farming, pastoralism and fishing.
